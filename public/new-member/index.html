01 — Welcome

Welcome to Omnia

Since 2013, Omnia has been a place where people become someone they didn't know was possible. We're a community that trains together and shows up for each other. Omnia isn't for everyone, but it could be everything for you.

We've built a gym where everyone belongs. Everyone starts somewhere, regardless of skill or fitness level, and everyone has room to grow. It's an honor to support you in that.

02 — What to expect

Fitness

We lay the foundation with expert coaching and programming. You bring the effort, a good attitude, humility, and a willingness to be a great neighbor. You get out what you put in.

This is a place where everyday people accomplish things they once thought were out of reach. Fitness should build confidence, resilience, and momentum that carries into every other part of life. Show up consistently, trust the process, and let us help you find out what you're capable of.

Culture

We take fitness and our community seriously — ourselves, not so much. You'll find hard work, encouragement, high-fives, and plenty of laughs. We push each other to improve while keeping a room where everyone, from complete beginners to experienced athletes, feels welcomed, respected, and supported.

Progress over perfection, 100% of the time. First day or tenth year, you'll be surrounded by people who want you to succeed.

Community

You're not just joining a gym. You're joining a community. Fitness is more fun, more meaningful, and more sustainable when you do it alongside people who know your name. Whatever season of life you're in, you'll have people beside you.

Great coaching and smart programming matter, but it's the people who make this place what it is. Members become friends, coaches become mentors, and Omnia starts to feel like a second home.

05 — Coaching services

Want to take things up a level?

If you're chasing bigger goals, our coaches build personalized services that meet you exactly where you are.

01

Nutrition coaching

If you want to lose weight, build muscle, improve your energy, or fuel your performance, nutrition is one of the most powerful tools you have. We help you build sustainable habits that fit your life, whether you just want to feel healthier or you're prepping for your next competition.

02

Individual programming

Got goals outside of class? Chasing your first strict pull-up, building strength, training for HYROX, prepping for a competition, or working around an injury? Individualized programming gives you a personal gameplan that complements your regular classes.

03

Skill sessions

We'll help you get your first kipping pull-up, muscle-up, handstand push-up, or double-under, and sharpen your Olympic lifts and gymnastics. One-on-one sessions give you dedicated time to build confidence, clean up technique, and speed up your progress.

04

Lifestyle & accountability

Fitness is about far more than one hour in the gym. Sleep, stress, mindset, habits, and consistency all shape your health. If you want extra accountability and guidance, we're here for it. The biggest breakthroughs usually happen in the habits you build between workouts.

05

Personal training

Not everyone is ready for group classes, and that's perfectly okay. Maybe you're brand new, coming back after time away, recovering from an injury, or you simply prefer one-on-one. Personal training is coaching tailored to your goals, abilities, and pace. For some it's a stepping stone into classes; for others it's the perfect long-term fit.
